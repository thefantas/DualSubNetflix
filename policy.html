Privacy Policy — Dual Subs for Netflix

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Dual Subs for Netflix is a browser extension that shows two subtitle languages at the same time on Netflix. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not sell or share data.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (languages, sizes, colours, shortcut behaviour), saved in the browser's local storage for netflix.com.
  • The interface language you choose, and — for interface languages other than English and Spanish — the translated text of the extension's own labels, saved in the extension's local storage so they are not translated again.

You can erase all of it by removing the extension or with Reset settings in its panel.

What is sent over the network, and to whom

  1. Netflix. Subtitle files are downloaded from Netflix's own servers, the same files the Netflix player uses. To find them, the extension reads the subtitle track list that Netflix's player already receives in the page. No other data is sent to Netflix by the extension.
  2. Google, only when the remote translation engine is in use. Machine translation is off until you turn it on. Once you do, the plain text of the subtitle lines around the current playback position is sent to Google's translation service (translate.googleapis.com), together with the source and target language codes, so it can be translated. Nothing else is sent: no account data, no viewing history, no title identifiers, no cookies. As with any web request, Google receives your IP address. Google's privacy policy applies to that service.
    • In Chrome and Chromium-based browsers, translation runs on your device with the browser's built-in Translator API, and the subtitle text does not leave your computer. The browser may download a language model from Google the first time. The remote engine is only used if you select it.
    • In Firefox, which has no on-device translation, the remote engine is the only one available and comes preselected.
  3. Interface translation. When you pick an interface language other than English or Spanish, the extension's own interface labels — not any of your data — are translated once, on your device when possible, otherwise through the same Google service.

The donation link

The popup contains a link to Buy Me a Coffee. Nothing is sent when the popup is open; the link only opens that site in a new tab if you click it, and from then on that site's own privacy policy applies. The extension does not know whether you donated, and donating unlocks nothing.
